# Store listings

Text for Google Play and the App Store. Both accounts belong to Runovate LLC; the apps are built by
NovaTech. Package and bundle id: `org.novatech.orbit`. Privacy policy:
`https://github.com/MohamedHussein811/orbit/blob/main/PRIVACY.md`.

## Google Play

- **App name** (30 max): Orbit: Private Web Browser
- **Short description** (80 max): Private browser that blocks ads and trackers. No accounts, no telemetry.
- **Category**: Communication (where Play lists browsers)
- **Full description**:

> Orbit is a private web browser built on Chromium.
>
> - Shields block ads, trackers and known malicious sites, with the filter lists built in.
> - DuckDuckGo is the default search engine. Google, Bing, Ecosia, Brave Search and Startpage are
>   one tap away.
> - Secure DNS through Quad9 and HTTPS-only mode are on from the start.
> - No accounts, no sync, no ads, no analytics, no crash reports, no telemetry. Orbit sends nothing
>   to its makers.
> - Sign-in, Safe Browsing lookups to Google, page prediction, translation, the browser's AI
>   features and the advertising web APIs are off by default.
>
> Your bookmarks, history and settings stay on your phone.

- **Data safety form**: no data collected, no data shared. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS for
  pages the person opens). No account creation, so no deletion request flow is needed.
- **Content rating**: questionnaire as a browser (unrestricted internet access: yes).
- **Ads**: the app contains no ads.
- **Target audience**: 18 and over (a browser reaches the whole web).

## App Store

- **Name** (30 max): Orbit: Private Web Browser ("Orbit Private Browser" is taken on the App Store)
- **Subtitle** (30 max): Blocks ads and trackers
- **Category**: Utilities (secondary: Productivity)
- **Keywords** (100 max): browser,private,privacy,ad blocker,tracker,duckduckgo,secure,web,incognito
- **Promotional text**: A private browser with built-in Shields. No accounts, no telemetry.
- **Description**:

> Orbit is a private web browser for iPhone.
>
> - Shields block ads, trackers and known malicious sites with built-in lists, before they load.
> - Private tabs keep nothing after they close.
> - DuckDuckGo search by default, with Google, Bing, Ecosia, Brave Search and Startpage to choose
>   from.
> - Bookmarks and history stay on your iPhone. No accounts, no sync, no analytics, no telemetry.
>   Orbit makes no connections of its own.

- **App Privacy**: Data Not Collected.
- **Export compliance**: uses only standard encryption (HTTPS through the system);
  `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is false in the app.
- **Review notes**: Orbit is a browser using WKWebView. Its own features: the Shields switch on the
  new tab page (content blocking through WKContentRuleList, about 157,000 rules compiled on first
  launch), private tabs in a separate non-persistent data store, local bookmarks and history, and a
  search engine choice. No login is needed.
- **Default browser**: after the first approval, request the default browser entitlement
  (`com.apple.developer.web-browser`) in the developer account; it has its own requirements.
